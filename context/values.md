# Values

## Real Life Over Screens

Planless exists to help people experience more of real life.

We don't want to build another platform that keeps people scrolling, watching, and consuming.

We want people to use Planless and then go outside, meet people, play, explore, eat, travel, and experience things.

---

## Doing Over Talking

We believe plans are valuable only when they actually happen.

Less:

- "We should do this."
- "Let's plan something."
- Endless group conversations.

More:

- Decide
- Commit
- Show up
- Do it

**Talk less. Do more.**

---

## People Over Content

Planless is built around people, not content.

The goal isn't to maximize screen time.

The goal is to create meaningful interactions between real people in the real world.

---

## Simplicity

Planning something with friends shouldn't feel like project management.

We remove unnecessary steps, decisions, conversations, and complexity.

**If it can be simpler, make it simpler.**

---

## Experiences Over Transactions

A booking is not the end goal.

The real value is the experience that happens after the booking.

Whether it's football, dinner, a movie, a trek, or something completely different, the transaction exists to enable the experience.

---

## People First

Build around how people actually behave, not how we think they should behave.

Listen to users.

Watch what they do.

Understand their problems.

Build accordingly.

---

## Make It Happen

We believe the hardest part of doing something together is often getting everyone aligned.

Planless should remove that friction and make action easier.

Every part of the product should answer:

> **Does this make it easier for people to actually do something?**

If not, question whether it needs to exist.

---

## Authenticity

Planless should feel human.

No fake urgency.

No unnecessary hype.

No pretending something is valuable when it isn't.

Be clear about what we do, what we don't do, and what we're still figuring out.

---

## Build With Reality

Ideas are hypotheses.

Users, behavior, and real-world usage are the source of truth.

Build.

Test.

Learn.

Improve.

Repeat.

---

## Community

Real-world experiences become better when people have people to share them with.

Planless should help people find, build, and participate in communities around shared interests and experiences.

---

## Progress Over Perfection

We should move quickly, but not carelessly.

Launch.

Learn.

Fix.

Improve.

Don't spend months perfecting something nobody wants.

---

## Long-Term Belief

Technology should make real life better, not replace it.

Planless exists to use technology to help people spend **less time coordinating online and more time living offline.**
