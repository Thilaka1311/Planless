This file defines the ideal customers for Planless. It describes who Planless should initially target, their behaviors, needs, problems, and why they are likely to use the product. AI should use this file to understand who Planless is building for when making product, marketing, growth, and business decisions.

# Ideal Customer Profile

## Primary Customer

Planless initially targets **organizers inside friend groups**.

These are the people who naturally take responsibility for making plans happen. They are usually the person who:

- Starts the plan
- Asks who is coming
- Decides the time and place
- Books the activity
- Tracks attendance
- Manages changes and cancellations
- Collects or splits money
- Coordinates the group until the plan happens

They are the entry point into Planless because one organizer can bring an entire group onto the platform.

---

## Core Characteristics

The ideal early user is:

- Socially active
- Frequently makes plans with friends
- Comfortable using digital products
- Part of one or more active friend groups
- Often responsible for coordinating group activities
- Frustrated by scattered conversations and coordination
- Willing to try a dedicated tool if it clearly makes planning easier

---

## Typical Plans

Examples include:

- Football or other sports
- Dinners
- Movies
- Trips
- Meetups
- Activities
- Events
- Group bookings
- Other real-world experiences involving multiple people

The specific activity is less important than the behavior:

> **They regularly need to get a group of people together and make something happen.**

---

## Current Behavior

The ideal customer often coordinates through messaging apps and other disconnected tools.

A typical planning process may involve:

**Conversation → availability → attendance → booking → payment → changes → execution**

This creates problems such as:

- Long conversations
- Unclear attendance
- Repeated questions
- Last-minute changes
- Difficulty managing waitlists
- Scattered payment information
- Coordination across multiple apps

Planless brings these activities into a structured **Plan**.

---

## Core Job To Be Done

The customer wants to:

> **Get a group of people together and make a real-world plan happen without having to constantly coordinate everything manually.**

Planless should reduce the coordination work rather than create another place the user has to manage.

---

## Why They Adopt Planless

The primary reason to use Planless is not simply that it has more features.

It should make the organizer think:

> **"This is easier than coordinating this on WhatsApp."**

The value comes from turning an unstructured conversation into an actionable Plan with:

- Participants
- Attendance
- Time
- Location
- Booking
- Expenses
- Waitlist
- Completion
- Memory
