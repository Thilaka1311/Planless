# Offer

## What We Sell

Planless sells a better way to organize and participate in real-world activities.

It turns a messy group conversation into a structured Plan where people can decide, join, coordinate, pay, book, and actually show up.

## The Core Offer

### For People

Planless helps people:

- Create plans in seconds
- Invite friends
- See who is actually coming
- Manage attendance
- Handle payments and expenses
- Manage waitlists
- Coordinate the details of an activity
- Discover things to do
- Join plans created by other people
- Keep a record of experiences

### Core Value

**Stop coordinating. Start doing.**

Instead of spending 50 messages figuring out a football game, dinner, movie, trip, or outing, create a Plan and get everyone on the same page.

---

## Initial Customer

### The Organizer

The primary initial customer is the person who usually organizes activities for their friend group.

They currently have to:

- Ask who's coming
- Decide the time
- Find a place
- Make the booking
- Collect money
- Handle cancellations
- Keep track of everything

Planless removes this coordination burden.

### The transformation

**Before Planless:**

"Guys, football Saturday?"

→ 40 messages  
→ people don't respond  
→ someone cancels  
→ nobody knows who paid  
→ organizer has to figure everything out

**With Planless:**

Create Plan  
→ Invite everyone  
→ People join  
→ Attendance is clear  
→ Payment is handled  
→ Booking is organized  
→ Everyone knows the plan

# Long-Term Offer

## For Businesses

Planless eventually sells businesses access to a network of people who are actively deciding what they want to do.

Businesses can:

- Create their own Plans
- Offer experiences
- Fill unused capacity
- Accept bookings
- Reach relevant local audiences
- Build communities around their business
- Understand demand and activity patterns
- Turn available inventory into real-world experiences

Examples:

- A turf creates an open football session.
- A restaurant creates a community dinner.
- A cinema creates a movie night.
- A tour company creates a weekend trek.
- An event organizer creates a public meetup.

The business is not simply a listing.

**The business becomes a participant in the network.**
