# Product

## What is Planless?

Planless is a social planning and coordination platform that helps people turn intentions into real-world activities.

It makes it easier to create a plan, invite people, get clear commitments, coordinate details, and actually make the activity happen.

The core object in Planless is the **Plan**.

A Plan connects:
- People
- Groups
- Activities
- Places
- Time
- Participation
- Payments and bookings when required

## Problem

People often want to do things together but struggle to actually organize them.

Planning through messaging apps creates:
- Long conversations
- Unclear decisions
- Uncertain attendance
- Repeated questions
- Payment confusion
- Last-minute cancellations
- Organizer burden

The problem is not the lack of ways to communicate.

The problem is turning communication into a clear commitment to actually do something.

## Core Product Idea

Planless turns:

**"We should do something."**

into:

**"It's happening."**

The product is designed around reducing coordination friction and increasing the likelihood that a planned activity actually happens.

## Core Loop

**Create → Invite → Decide → Coordinate → Do → Remember**

1. **Create** — Someone creates a Plan.
2. **Invite** — Relevant people are invited.
3. **Decide** — People clearly join or skip.
4. **Coordinate** — Details, participants, payments, and bookings are handled.
5. **Do** — The activity happens in the real world.
6. **Remember** — The experience can become part of the user's history and memories.

## Core Product Principles

### Real Life Over Screens
Planless uses technology to help people spend more time doing things offline.

### Doing Over Talking
The product should help people move from conversation to commitment and action.

### People Over Content
The primary value comes from people participating in experiences together, not consuming content.

### Simplicity
Creating and joining a plan should require as little effort as possible.

### Commitment
The product should make it clear who is actually participating.

### Experiences Over Transactions
Bookings and payments exist to enable real-world experiences, not to become the primary purpose of the product.

## Primary User

The initial user is the **organizer** inside a friend group.

This is the person who usually:
- Starts the plan
- Asks who is coming
- Finds the place
- Decides the time
- Makes bookings
- Collects money
- Handles cancellations
- Keeps everyone updated

Planless reduces this coordination burden.

## Core User Experience

A user should be able to:

- Create a Plan quickly
- Invite relevant people
- See who is participating
- Join or skip clearly
- Understand the important details immediately
- Manage participation
- Handle payments when required
- Manage waitlists when necessary
- Coordinate the activity
- Participate in the real-world experience

## Plans

The **Plan** is the fundamental product object.

A Plan represents a specific real-world activity.

Examples:
- Football match
- Badminton game
- Basketball game
- Dinner
- Movie
- Cafe visit
- Trip
- Trek
- Event
- Meetup

A Plan can contain information such as:
- Activity
- Date
- Time
- Location
- Host
- Participants
- Capacity
- Cost
- Payment status
- Booking status
- Participation status
- Plan status

## Circles

A **Circle** is a group of people who regularly interact or participate in activities together.

Examples:
- Matchday
- College Friends
- Movie Club
- Cafe Hop
- Weekend Crew

Plans can be created within Circles.

Circles provide the recurring social context around Plans.

## Participation

Participation should be explicit.

Users should be able to clearly understand:
- Who is attending
- Who is not attending
- Whether the Plan is full
- Whether they are waitlisted
- Whether payment is required
- Whether their participation is confirmed

The system should minimize ambiguity around attendance.

## Payments & Bookings

Some Plans require money or external bookings.

Planless can support:
- Participant payments
- Shared expenses
- Entry fees
- Booking-related payments
- Payment status
- Refunds when applicable
- Waitlists when capacity is reached

Payments and bookings should support the Plan rather than distract from the core experience.

## Social Layer

Planless is a social product, but its social layer is centered around **real-world activity**.

The primary social unit is not a post.

It is a **Plan**.

The product should encourage:
- Meeting people
- Doing activities together
- Creating recurring groups
- Participating in shared experiences
- Building communities around activities

## Product Direction

Planless starts as a coordination product for people and their existing social groups.

Over time, it can become a broader real-world social network where:
- People create Plans
- Communities organize activities
- Businesses create experiences
- People discover things to do
- Businesses provide places and experiences
- Plans connect demand with supply

The long-term direction is to become a social layer for real-world experiences.

## What Planless Is Not

Planless is not primarily:
- A messaging app
- A generic event calendar
- An event listing website
- A passive social feed
- A booking marketplace
- A content platform

These may support the product, but they are not the core product.

## Core Product Promise

**Less planning. More doing.**

Planless should make it easier for people to go from:

**Intent → Commitment → Real-world experience.**
