# Business Info

- Name: Planless
- Category: Social planning and coordination platform
- Core idea: Help people turn "we should do this" into an actual plan.
- Planless is built around a simple idea: people want to do things together, but coordinating them is difficult.
- The platform brings planning, people, bookings, attendance, expenses, and real-world experiences into one place.

## The Problem

- Most group plans happen through WhatsApp, Instagram, calls, and scattered booking apps.
- Conversations become messy and decisions get buried.
- People don't know who is coming.
- Plans change constantly.
- Someone usually has to coordinate everyone.
- Payments and bookings become another source of friction.
- Plans often die because coordination is harder than the activity itself.
- Existing social platforms are primarily designed for communication or content consumption, not getting people to actually do things.

## The Solution

- Planless turns group coordination into a structured Plan.
- A Plan can contain:
  - What people are doing
  - When they are doing it
  - Where they are going
  - Who is coming
  - Who has joined or dropped
  - Booking information
  - Expenses and payments
  - Waitlists
  - Completion and memories
- The goal is to remove unnecessary coordination and increase the likelihood that plans actually happen.

## Core Product

- The fundamental object in Planless is the **Plan**.
- People create Plans around activities and experiences.
- Friends join Plans and coordinate through them.
- Plans can eventually be private, community-based, or public.
- The product starts with friend-group coordination and can expand into broader real-world social discovery.

## Initial Customer

- The initial focus is on the **organizer inside a friend group**.
- This is the person who normally asks:
  - "Who's coming?"
  - "What time?"
  - "Where?"
  - "Who is booking?"
  - "Send me the money."
- One organizer can bring an entire group onto Planless.
- This creates a natural path for user-to-user growth.

## Long-Term Business

- Planless can expand from people creating Plans to businesses creating Plans.
- Businesses can create experiences, events, sessions, and available inventory.
- Examples:
  - Turfs
  - Restaurants
  - Cinemas
  - Events
  - Tours
  - Classes
  - Other experience-based businesses
- Businesses can use Plans to attract customers, fill unused capacity, organize experiences, and manage demand.
- This creates a network connecting people, businesses, and real-world experiences.

## How Planless Makes Money

- Booking commissions
- Transaction fees
- Business services
- Business-created experiences
- Potential premium business tools
- Potential partnerships and integrations
- Other revenue generated from transactions and activity happening through the platform

## Business Model

- People create demand.
- Businesses provide experiences and supply.
- Plans connect people and businesses.
- Planless facilitates the coordination, discovery, and transaction.
- As activity on the platform grows, the value of the network increases.

## Long-Term Vision

- Planless should become a social network built around **real-world experiences**, not passive content consumption.
- People should use Planless to discover what to do, find people to do it with, organize it, attend it, and remember it.
- Businesses should eventually be able to participate directly in this network.
- The long-term goal is to become a platform where people and businesses meet through Plans.

## Core Principle

**Less coordinating. More doing.**

Planless exists to help people spend less time figuring out plans and more time actually experiencing them.
